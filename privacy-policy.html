Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 July 2026

A+Connect (we, us, our) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you interact with our website, submit forms, or purchase our services. We comply with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa.

Our commitment: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with any third-party marketing companies. Your data is used only to serve you better.

1. What Information We Collect

We collect only the information you voluntarily provide via our forms:

  • Contact & Enquiry Forms: Name, email address, subject, and message.
  • Review Forms: Name, email address, rating (1–5), and your feedback comment.
  • Template Booking / Purchase: Name, email address, chosen template ID, and reference number.
  • Automatically Collected: IP address, browser type, and pages visited (via cookies) to improve our site performance. We use minimal analytics and do not share this with advertisers.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide customer support.
  • To process your template bookings and set up your website.
  • To publish approved reviews (anonymously if requested) to help other clients.
  • To send you service-related emails (e.g., booking confirmation, invoice, updates). We do not send marketing spam.
  • To improve our website and services based on usage patterns.

3. Where We Store Your Data

Your data is stored securely on Supabase (a cloud database platform) which uses industry-standard encryption. Supabase’s servers may be located in regions outside South Africa (e.g., United States or Europe).

By submitting your information, you explicitly consent to this cross-border transfer of your personal data. We have taken reasonable steps to ensure that Supabase provides adequate protection for your data.

4. Data Security

  • All data transmitted to our site is protected via HTTPS (SSL encryption).
  • Access to our Supabase database is restricted via secure API keys and role-based permissions.
  • We regularly update our systems to protect against vulnerabilities.
  • Despite these measures, no online transmission is 100% secure – we cannot guarantee absolute security but we strive to protect your data.

5. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Contact messages: 12 months, after which they are permanently deleted.
  • Reviews: We keep approved reviews indefinitely to maintain social proof. You may request deletion at any time.
  • Booking records: 7 years for tax and legal compliance (retained in a secure archive).
  • You may request earlier deletion of your data by contacting us (see Section 8).

6. Your Rights (POPIA)

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Ask us to correct any inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal data (unless we are legally required to retain it).
  • Object: Object to the processing of your data for specific purposes.
  • Withdraw consent: Withdraw your consent at any time (for future processing).

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@aplustconnect.com with your request. We will respond within 7 working days.

7. Cookies

We use minimal cookies to remember your preferences and to analyse site traffic. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect some functionality.
